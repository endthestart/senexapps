About SENEX

A small suite of apps that run on your own machines, made by Michael Anderson.

They share one set of promises: your files stay on your own computers, nothing phones home, and the code is open.

The names

The photo and music apps are named for constellations and carry them in their icons: Pictor, the Painter's Easel; Corvus, the Crow; and Lyra, the Lyre.

Towpath and Poundlock are named for canals. A towpath is the path beside the water, and a pound lock is the chamber with a gate at each end that lifts a boat from one level to the next.
