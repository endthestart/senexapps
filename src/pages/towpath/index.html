Towpath · The path beside the canal

Your digital life, searchable on your own server.

Towpath is a self-hosted workspace that finds what is in your mail, files and exports, helps you organize it, and builds a timeline that cites its sources. Email is the first working source.

Read the Install Guide Source on GitHub

For one owner on a home server, with Docker Compose and an HTTPS reverse proxy.

Find it, tidy it, remember it. Nothing changes until you allow it.

  • Discovery

    One search over your mail accounts, folders, archives and old mail backups, indexed with Recoll. Paperless-ngx and Immich are read for lookups.

  • Mail management

    Towpath proposes changes over IMAP. A separate runner carries out only what you asked for or approved, and each kind of action is a dry run until you turn it on.

  • Life stream

    Finds events in the mail you allow, gathers them into memories, and answers questions with the sources cited.

  • Your accounts

    Gmail, read-only, with your own OAuth client. IMAP mail from Fastmail, iCloud, Yahoo or Gmail. CardDAV and CalDAV contacts and calendars. Facebook and Apple Messages exports.

  • Models under policy

    Model features use only the OpenAI-compatible endpoints you configure, such as Poundlock. Nothing goes to a model you haven't granted.

  • Set up in the browser

    One data folder and one Compose file. Accounts, folders, passwords and indexing are all added in the browser afterwards.
