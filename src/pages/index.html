The same promises. In every app, and on this site.

  • On your machines

    Your photos, music, mail and code stay on your own computers and drives. There is no SENEX account and no SENEX cloud.

  • Nothing phones home

    No telemetry, analytics or ads, in the apps or here. An app uses the network only for the job you give it, such as reading your mail or looking up an album.

  • Open source

    Six are on GitHub under MIT or Apache 2.0. Lyra isn't published yet.
