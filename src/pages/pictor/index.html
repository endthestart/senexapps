SENEX Pictor · The Painter's Easel

A night of sky, stacked over a sharp foreground.

Pictor turns a folder of RAW frames from a night shoot into one finished image. The sky is stacked for clean, deep stars; the ground keeps the exposure and sharpness you shot it with.

See How It Works In development

No public build yet. macOS first, then Linux and Windows.

Six steps, one window. Each screen shows why, not just what.

  • Import

    Drop a folder of RAW files. Pictor finds the runs, sets aside lit and overexposed frames, and checks lens, colour, memory and disk space before anything is written. Cancel at any time and resume from the last finished stage.

  • Frames

    See why each frame was used or left out: lit, lost, how far it drifted, and a blink against the reference.

  • Sky & ground

    Tint the sky and its boundary over the result with O, then paint corrections as sky or ground with S and G.

  • Scene

    Build a hero composite around one frame, or a moment from a short window of the night, and open it straight away.

  • Compare

    Two renderings side by side. Space swaps them in place, S splits the view, and 200 % crops take A, same or B verdicts.

  • Look & export

    Tune the look live, then export a linear 32-bit master, a 16-bit TIFF, a JPEG or an HDR JPEG, with an HDR preview on displays that show light above paper white.

The same engine on the command line. For batches and scripts.

pictor process ~/Nights/2026-10-12 ~/Nights/2026-10-12-out

One command checks the folder, picks the recommended run, aligns, stacks and renders final.jpg and final.tif. Run it again into the same folder and it carries on where it stopped.
