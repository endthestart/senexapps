Privacy

Nothing about you is collected, on this site or in the apps.

This site

  • No cookies, analytics, ads or tracking pixels.
  • No third-party scripts, fonts or embeds: every file comes from senexapps.com.
  • Like any web server, the one that hosts these pages receives your IP address and the page you ask for.

The apps

  • Every app runs on your own machines. There is no SENEX account and no SENEX cloud service, and none of them has telemetry or analytics.
  • Corvus reads the cards and folders you point it at and writes only to the destination you choose. macOS asks you to allow its access to removable and network volumes and to cameras.
  • Pictor reads the RAW folder you open and writes only to the output folder you choose.
  • Lyra works in the music folders you name. Identifying an album sends its details to MusicBrainz.
  • Towpath keeps its data in one folder on your server. It connects to the mail, contact and calendar accounts you add, and sends content to a model only through an endpoint you configure and allow.
  • Poundlock sends prompts and files to the model servers you connect. A model outside your network is used only when you grant that access, within the budget you set.
  • Poundlock Dispatch keeps conversations, plans and settings on your machine. Prompts and the files its models read go to your Poundlock controller.
  • Poundlock Toolkit runs on the machine or SSH target you choose and writes its reports to files you name.
