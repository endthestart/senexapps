Privacy

Nothing about you is collected, on this site or in the apps.

This site

  • No cookies, analytics, ads or tracking pixels.
  • No third-party scripts, fonts or embeds: every file comes from senexapps.com.
  • Like any web server, the one that hosts these pages receives your IP address and the page you ask for.

The apps

  • Pictor and Corvus run on your Mac. There is no account and no cloud service, and they don't upload your photos.
  • They have no telemetry or analytics.
  • Corvus reads the cards and folders you point it at and writes only to the destination you choose. macOS asks you to allow its access to removable and network volumes and to cameras.
  • Pictor reads the RAW folder you open and writes only to the output folder you choose.
