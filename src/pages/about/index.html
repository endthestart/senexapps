About SENEX

A small suite of photo apps for the Mac, made by Michael Anderson.

Each app is named for a constellation and carries it in its icon: Pictor, the Painter's Easel, and Corvus, the Crow. They share one dark look, built for judging photos, and one set of promises: your photos stay on your Mac, nothing phones home, and the code is open.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub for Pictor or Corvus. Everything else is on GitHub.
