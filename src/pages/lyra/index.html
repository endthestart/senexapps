SENEX Lyra · The Lyre

A music collection, put in order.

Lyra finds what you have, identifies the albums, files them in one library under one naming scheme, and sets duplicates aside without deleting anything.

See What It Does In development

Not released yet: there is no download or public source. It runs on macOS, Linux and Windows.

From folders to a library. Every change is shown first and can be undone.

  • Inventory

    Reads every folder you name: tags, format and quality, where each copy came from, incomplete albums and exact duplicates. Audio checks find lossy transcodes and fake 24-bit files.

  • Identification

    Looks each album up on MusicBrainz through beets. Near-certain matches are applied; the others wait for you. An album nothing matches can be filed as it is.

  • One library

    Identified albums are named by one scheme and, if you want, sorted into category folders such as Albums, Classical and Compilations, or by your own rules.

  • Duplicates and versions

    Of two copies of an album the better one stays, decided by a version policy you can read. The other moves to a quarantine folder, and its tags move to the copy that stays.

  • Review

    Each planned change is a card that says what moves where and why. Approve one, a page or a whole tab.

  • Nothing deleted

    Removed copies wait in the quarantine folder and can be restored. Every job can be undone, files and tags alike.
